Urdu Clip Machine

The problem
A half-hour talk show contains about one minute worth posting. Finding that minute, recutting it vertical, keeping the speaker centred and captioning it in Urdu takes roughly 40 minutes by hand, twice a day.
What it does
You email a YouTube link and the timestamp you want. The finished clip comes back by email: 3:4, face-centred, the channel's news ticker removed, Urdu captions burned in. Nobody opens an editor. Nobody approves anything.
The hard part
Standard subtitle rendering cannot draw Nastaliq — it produces empty boxes. The captions are rendered as images with a shaping engine and composited onto the frames instead. Glyphs overhang their own measured box in Nastaliq, so the text is drawn from the measured ink bounds, not the canvas edge.

Lead Capture Pipeline

The problem
Orders arrive as a form, someone retypes them into a spreadsheet, and the ones with a broken email address quietly disappear.
What it does
Form to validation to spreadsheet to notification email, with the order routed by type. Submissions that fail validation are rejected with an explanation and logged separately, so nothing is lost silently.
Why it matters
This is the shape most small businesses need first. It was rebuilt from an empty canvas without notes, which is the only real test of whether you understand a workflow.

Home Telemetry & Alerts

The problem
A solar inverter, an air conditioner and two IP cameras on one network, none of them able to tell you when something is wrong.
What it does
Small HTTP bridges read each device on the LAN and expose one clean endpoint each. n8n polls them and emails when battery voltage drops, when load spikes, or when the voltage reads wrong. The air-conditioner controller and the camera watchdog are built and tested but currently switched off; the inverter is deliberately read-only.
Why read-only
Because writing to an inverter can damage it, and no automation is worth that. Deciding what a system must never do is part of designing it.

How I work

Three habits, and the reason for each.

Evidence, not estimates

Every number on this page came out of a log, a database or a measured run. If I have not checked something, I say so instead of rounding it into a claim.

Built node by node

No imported templates. I can open any workflow here and rebuild it from an empty canvas, which means I can also repair it when it breaks at an awkward hour.

The failure path first

Every pipeline here has a route for the run that fails, and it reports the actual reason. A system that only works when everything is fine is not finished.
